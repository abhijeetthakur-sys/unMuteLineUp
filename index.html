    New month, new city.

    1. Audition

      Artium learners audition for a place on the lineup.

    2. Perform

      Each edition is one evening in one city, in front of a live audience. Learners and their teachers perform side by side.

    3. Release

      Every set is professionally recorded and produced, then published on Artium’s YouTube channel.
